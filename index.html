THE STORY BEHIND THE NAME

Remembering Archbishop Joseph Kiwanuka.

Joseph Nakabaale Kiwanuka is central to the identity of Kitovu. Masaka Diocese records that he was ordained a priest in 1929, appointed Bishop of Masaka in 1939, and moved the diocesan headquarters from Villa Maria to Kitovu in 1940. He later became Archbishop of Rubaga in 1961 and took part in the Second Vatican Council.

His legacy is especially meaningful for a school: faith, education, formation, service and the development of people who can contribute to society. Naming the school after him connects today's learners with that wider Ugandan and Catholic educational heritage rooted in Kitovu.

“The leadership of Archbishop Kiwánuka opened the door to the full development of the Catholic Church in Africa.”

Historical account: Masaka Diocese
